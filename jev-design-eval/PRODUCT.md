# Purpose

A synthetic workshop reservation app used to evaluate browser navigation and design QA.
The user requested a complex single-page fixture with broken and valid flows.

## Register

Product. A coordinator books workshop seats at a desk in daylight or on a phone while moving between rooms.
Use a light, neutral interface, restrained coral actions, legible system typography, and compact event rows.
The brand is fictional. All data stays in the local browser. No payments or messages are sent.

## Evaluation scope

Filter sessions, inspect details, reserve seats, enter attendee data, apply an access code,
and open the reservation review. Include approved design exceptions and seeded rendering defects.
Frozen requirements and independent browser checks define the answer key.
Jev receives observed measurements and requirements, never variant identities or expected answers.
